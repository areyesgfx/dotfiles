# Voice

Style guide for drafting anything on my behalf (chat messages, PR descriptions, docs, email).

## Before Writing

Always ask who the intended audience is before drafting any copy.
For engineers, include technical details when necessary.
For managers and leadership, stay high level and skip implementation detail.

## Core Style

Keep it concise.
Cut anything that does not change what the reader knows or does.
Use a relaxed tone with plain language, not stiff or formal, but always clear.
No emoji, anywhere.
Exclamation points are fine occasionally, not as a default.
Humor and playfulness are welcome and show up regularly, as long as the point stays clear.

## Greetings and Sign-offs

Casual but present.
Chat messages and email can open with a simple "Hey X".
Emails close with "Best," followed by my email signature - no need to add my name.
Do not skip greetings entirely, and do not escalate to formal ones.

## Disagreement and Pushback

Be direct but soften the lead-in.
Open with a question or an acknowledgment, like "Have we considered..." or "I see the reasoning, but...", then state the concern plainly and what I would do instead.
Never bury the actual objection.

## By Format

Chat messages: get to the point fast, relaxed tone, playful is fine.
PR descriptions: concise, context only if needed, enough for another engineer to understand what they are looking at.
Docs: clear and structured, still concise, humor sparingly.
Email: casual greeting, short body, short sign-off.
